## Timing note

PI-SPI opened to the public in **April 2026**. In every comparable market (UK
Faster Payments, Pix, UPI) authorised-push-payment fraud ramped sharply **12–24
months after launch**, putting the WAEMU surge around **mid-2027 → 2028**.

Consequence: urgency is low *now* (participants are focused on connectivity and
adoption), so do not expect fraud budget yet. The play is to land one or two
**design partners now on the low-friction wedge** — the on-device anti-scam
banner, days to integrate, shadow mode — to hold the reference customer, the
tuned thresholds and the real fraud data when budgets open in 2027.

## Caveats

- Segment labels are **not** in the BCEAO PDF; EMI/MFI classification below is
  inferred from entity names. The totals reconcile (11 MFI, ~9 EMI, 59 banks),
  but which single entry BCEAO counts as the "1 payment institution" is unclear
  (Touchpoint is the likely candidate).
- Group ownership/footprint notes come from general knowledge and **need
  verification** before outreach — several of these have been restructuring
  (Société Générale's Africa portfolio, Orange's banking entities).
